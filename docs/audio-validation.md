## What runs

`game/sound.gd` is connected once to the existing destruction signals in BOTH
`destruction_demo.tscn` and the playable combat/opposition slice `opposition.tscn`.
The controls-only default scene remains controls-only: this task does not own
node 7 session objectives or change the default scene into a purported full game.
No audio is synthesized from a timer guessing when a fragment should land.
Real GodotPhysics solver observations feed the accepted impact signal.

Ten original MIT PCM assets: four layered fracture attacks and six short debris
impacts, regenerated by `scripts/generate_sound_assets.py`. Mathematical swept
low oscillators, noise crack/grit and inharmonic ringing only; no third-party
samples, downloads or model-generated recordings. Source seeds/hashes/license
are in `game/audio/provenance.json`; WAV import settings explicitly disable lossy
compression. Regeneration was exercised and all WAV bytes match exactly.

Fractures layer a 1.5ms attack with descending low body, crack and grit/tail.
The elevated arena camera is compensated with 50m unit distance, spatial stereo
panning and inverse-distance attenuation without a second distance fade. Initial
weaker tuning had a default single peak of 0.0905; final single peak is 0.3458.
That was signal-based tuning, NOT a claim that either version was heard.

Eight preallocated AudioStreamPlayer3D slots, no per-impact node creation.
Prefer free slots, then steal oldest impact; impacts never steal fracture
attacks. Only burst overload may steal the oldest fracture. Twelve simultaneous
breaks deliberately exceed eight slots: not every overlapping request gets a
separately sustained voice. Godot supplies short stop fades. No indefinitely
queued sounds. Clear/restart stops playback and clears throttling history.

Contact threshold >=1m/s; service global minimum three physics ticks and per-body
0.12s (eight 60Hz ticks). Sound defensively checks those bounds again, rejects
NaN/unknown materials, expires per-body history and varies sample and pitch with
no immediate sample repeat. Squared impact speed controls bounded energy/gain;
stronger collisions get louder/heavier impacts. Fractures have priority over grit.
Dedicated SFX bus has a -2dB hard limiter, zero pre-gain, 100ms release. User gains
cannot exceed unity. Pool cap/throttles limit density before the safety limiter.

Bottom-right Master/SFX sliders and M mute; minus/equal adjust SFX. They work
while paused and persist in user://sound.cfg. Zero is a genuine mute. The GUI
clears the input collector fire latch for its own clicks, preserving normal
WASD/mouse/LMB/dash/Esc/R behavior without firing while dragging audio settings.
Pause freezes active streams; restart and fixture N clear them.

## Playback exercised; listening limitation is explicit

Physical playback attempted with `aplay -D hw:0 runtime.wav`: failed, no sound
cards. /dev/snd exposes sequencer/timer only, not a playable sound device.
A private temporary PulseAudio daemon supplied a named NULL sink. `paplay`
successfully sent the delivered WAV to it; `parec` captured its monitor. A fresh
normal Linux game using PulseAudio also drove that monitor while running the
same real destruction/physics tests. The private daemon was then stopped.

Monitor sample peaks were 0.794342 (WAV playback) and 0.797668 (live game), with
zero full-scale clipping. These prove nonzero virtual transport, NOT listening.
An added full-recording fixed-offset waveform-fidelity diagnostic FAILED:
normalized correlation only 0.12696. The loopback is not accepted as lossless or
clock-faithful. The simultaneous external FFmpeg video also dropped frames,
encoding only 53, so it is NOT used to validate synchronization or smoothness.
Both limitations are preserved explicitly in the checker report and raw logs.
The actual MovieWriter common-clock video/PCM is the primary verified sync path.

What was actually heard by this worker: NOTHING. No reliable auditory perception
or physical speaker path is available. Successful paplay, a WAV, sample peaks,
and nonzero monitor data are not proof of satisfying audible output. No claim
of clear, forceful, satisfying subjective sound, visual quality or fun is made.
The human exact-release Linux playtest must listen on real speakers/headphones,
judge attack/weight/grit/variation, crowded mix and limiter pumping, sensible
volume, mute/pause transitions and latency. Visually judge debris readability
and weight there as well. This is a specific handoff, not fabricated acceptance.
